Hi, I'm Vanessa Senethong.

a new creative and technical developer

I recently graduated at the University of Colorado Boulder with a major in Computer Science and a minor in Creative Technology and Design.

I am interested in pursuing a career as a software engineer with an emphasis on human-computer interaction. Accessibility in technology is important to me, and as technology moves forward, our generation should be able to grow alongside it.

Outside of my academics, I am very involved with the Asian community here at CU, and I've had executive board positions in Asian Unity and Vietnamese Student Association for the entirety of my college career. I also like to travel and go to concerts (both while making many friends) in my free time!
